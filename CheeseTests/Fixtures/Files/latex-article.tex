\section{Discussion}
Extraction from typeset documents is difficult because the typesetter places words by
kerning rather than by inserting space characters. Ligatures such as fi, fl, ff, ffi
and ffl collapse several letters into one glyph, so a naive extractor reports
``efficient'' as a word with a missing letter. Our fluent approach fixes the affected
officers' documents.
